What we do

03A little prep

Before you come

December desert nights get cold fast once you sit still.

  1. Dress for near-freezing temperatures.
  2. Wear closed-toe shoes.
  3. Bring a warm hat and gloves.
  4. Bring water.
  5. Bring any medications you might need. Help can be far away.
  6. Arrive with a full gas tank (there’s no fuel in the park) and a car in good shape for night driving.
  7. Drive slowly after dark. Wildlife crosses park roads.
  8. Plan for the park entrance fee, or bring your own valid park pass.
  9. Download offline maps, since cell service is limited.
  10. Use red lights only.
  11. Watch for cactus and uneven ground.

Conditions change. Check current park alerts before you go.
